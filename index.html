QUESTIONS.

Do you offer on-site visits in Beirut?

Yes. We provide on-site support throughout Beirut and surrounding areas. For urgent issues we can typically arrive within 2–4 hours. Remote support is also available for faster resolution of software issues.

How fast is remote support?

Our average response time is under 10 minutes during business hours. Once connected, most issues are resolved within 30–60 minutes depending on complexity.

Can you support multiple offices?

Yes. We support businesses with several locations across Lebanon. Remote support reaches any site instantly, and we coordinate on-site visits as needed.

What's included in CCTV maintenance?

Regular system health checks, camera cleaning and alignment, storage checks and firmware updates. Faulty equipment is replaced quickly under the Business and Enterprise plans.

Is there a long-term contract?

No long-term contract on Starter. Business and Enterprise have flexible terms with 30-day notice.
